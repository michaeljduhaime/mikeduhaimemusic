About

Mike Duhaime has been writing and recording since 2018, working across country, folk, indie, and Americana. The sound pulls from Austin's live music roots with threads of Carolina twang and West Coast warmth—singer-songwriter stuff that doesn't sit still in one genre.

With the Howlers, it gets bigger. Pedal steel, electric and acoustic guitars, keys, bass, drums, and three-part harmonies that tie it all together. Any given night could be a loud southern rock set or a quiet folk show—depends on the room.
